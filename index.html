About

I am an incoming Eric and Wendy Schmidt Postdoctoral Fellow at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard. I completed my PhD in Mathematics at the University of British Columbia, under the supervision of Geoffrey Schiebinger and Yaniv Plan. My research is centered on developing statistical and computational methods for analyzing high-dimensional biological data.
